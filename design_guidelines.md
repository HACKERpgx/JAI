# JAI Website Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Inspiration:** xAI's Grok website aesthetic (2025) - futuristic minimalism with premium polish and subtle animations.

## Core Visual Identity

### Background & Mode
- Default dark mode with `#0f0f0f` or `#121212` background
- Subtle animated gradient accents in cyan and purple
- Very subtle particle background or light grid pattern (like Grok/xAI)
- Plenty of negative space for clean, premium feel

### Typography
- **Hero Headline "JAI":** Massive, bold custom sans-serif (similar to Neue Haas Grotesk or GT America)
- **Tagline:** "The world's most powerful and honest AI assistant"
- Clean, readable typography throughout with strong hierarchy

### Layout System
- Mobile-first design optimized for iOS (iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone SE) and Android (Pixel 8, Samsung Galaxy S24, foldables)
- Fully responsive with fluid breakpoints for desktop
- Perfect alignment and spacing throughout (Apple-level polish)
- Use Tailwind spacing: p-4, p-6, p-8, p-12, p-16, p-20 for consistent rhythm

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section
- Bold "JAI" headline centered at top
- Tagline underneath: "The world's most powerful and honest AI assistant"
- Generous vertical spacing

### 2. Interactive Button Row (Grok-style)
8 distinctive pill-shaped buttons with identical visual treatment:
1. **"Ask JAI anything"** - Primary CTA with bright cyan/blue glow
2. **"Try JAI on iOS"** - with Apple App Store badge
3. **"Try JAI on Android"** - with Google Play Store badge
4. **"JAI Pro"** - subscription button
5. **"API Access"**
6. **"Voice Mode"**
7. **"Image Generation"**
8. **"Fun Mode"**

**Button Specifications:**
- Large, pill-shaped with slightly rounded corners
- Subtle glow/border effect
- Monochrome icons on the left of text
- Soft hover glow and micro-animations
- Match exact visual style of Grok's 2025 website buttons

### 3. Feature Cards Section
Three-column grid showcasing:
- **"Reasoning"** - with icon and description
- **"Real-time Knowledge"** - with icon and description
- **"No Filter Honesty"** - with icon and description

Clean cards with icons, strong typography hierarchy

### 4. Mobile App Mockup Section
- Side-by-side iPhone and Android phone mockups
- Clean, borderless device frames
- Show JAI mobile app running on both devices
- Professional presentation quality

### 5. Footer
**Links:** About, Careers, Privacy, Terms  
**Social Icons:** Twitter/X, GitHub  
Clean, minimal footer design

## Animations & Interactions
- Subtle animated gradients (cyan/purple)
- Button hover: soft glow effects
- Micro-interactions throughout
- Very subtle parallax or scroll effects
- Particle/grid background animation (very subtle)
- Smooth transitions, Apple-level polish

## Images
**Mobile Device Mockups:** Clean, borderless iPhone and Android phone images showing the JAI app interface in the mockup section. Professional, studio-quality presentation.

**Hero:** No large hero image - focus on bold typography and animated gradient background with particle/grid effects.
